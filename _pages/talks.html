A PERSONAL POSTSCRIPT

A life in words.

In 2012, my high-school teacher inaugurated my first poetry book, Anthargami, in Chikmagalur, Karnataka. Writing began for me in Kannada, but it does not stay there alone. I also write in English, and I am deeply drawn to philosophy, interior life, and the strange emotional worlds that literature opens up.
