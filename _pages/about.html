Hello, I'm Kavana.

I'm a Computer Science PhD student at Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Jiaming Cui and affiliated with the Sanghani Center. I'm also a PRISM AI Safety Research Fellow.

MY RESEARCH · IN BRIEF

I study multi-agent safety and coordination, self-evolving agents, and emergent behaviors in LLM-based multi-agent systems. My work investigates how agents communicate, delegate, and develop collective patterns; how failures propagate through memory, tools, and communication; and how adaptive agent harnesses can help us monitor, evaluate, and safeguard increasingly autonomous systems.

More broadly, I'm interested in the relationship between agent autonomy, collective behavior, and system-level reliability—both empirically and through methods that make these systems more interpretable and dependable.

I have also worked on generative vision and diffusion models, exploring rare-concept image generation, personalized and creative editing, and ways of combining language and visual reasoning.

Before starting my PhD, I earned an M.S. in Data Science from Northeastern University in Boston and spent roughly two and a half years working as a data scientist.

Outside the lab, I'm also a writer and editor. Poetry, philosophy, photography, and bringing people together to learn are all part of this little world.
