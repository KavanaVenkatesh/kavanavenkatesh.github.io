abstract: >
  Text-to-image (T2I) diffusion models have made remarkable strides in generating and editing 
  high-fidelity images from text. Yet, these models remain fundamentally generic, failing to 
  adapt to the nuanced aesthetic preferences of individual users. In this work, we present 
  the first framework for personalized image editing in diffusion models, introducing 
  Collaborative Direct Preference Optimization (C-DPO), a novel method that aligns image 
  edits with user-specific preferences while leveraging collaborative signals from 
  like-minded individuals.
