## Hero copy

**Hello, I'm Kavana.**

I'm a Computer Science PhD student at Virginia Tech, advised by Dr. Jiaming Cui and affiliated with the Sanghani Center. I'm also a PRISM AI Safety Research Fellow.

**MY RESEARCH · IN BRIEF**

I study **multi-agent safety and coordination**, **self-evolving agents**, and **emergent behaviors in LLM-based multi-agent systems**. My work investigates how agents communicate, delegate, and develop collective patterns; how failures propagate through memory, tools, and communication; and how adaptive agent harnesses can help us monitor, evaluate, and safeguard increasingly autonomous systems.

More broadly, I'm interested in the relationship between **agent autonomy, collective behavior, and system-level reliability**—both empirically and through methods that make these systems more interpretable and dependable.

*Outside the lab, I'm also a writer and editor. Poetry, philosophy, photography, and bringing people together to learn are all part of this little world.*

## Research notebook

1. **Safety & coordination** — cascading attacks, causal attribution, security and reliability across memory, tools and communication.
2. **Self-evolving agents** — capability changes in skills, workflows, tools, and memory; safety adaptation as systems evolve.
3. **Emergent MAS behavior** — heavy-tailed interactions, empirical coordination laws, emergent influence, scaling bottlenecks.
4. **Harnesses & evaluation** — orchestration, monitoring, controlled interventions, and assessments of reliability, calibration, and uncertainty.

## Visual principles

- One portrait with a single mint backing sheet, not a pile of cards.
- Vintage typewriter labels are **accents**; technical paragraphs use readable proportional type.
- Scholar, LinkedIn, GitHub, Email, and CV links are immediately beneath the portrait.
- Research substance leads. The literary personality is visible but doesn't obscure scholarship.
- Motion is responsive to user interaction, not autoplay marketing animation.
